# Accelerator Readiness

Use for program selection, applications, and interviews. Apply [honest-evaluation.md](honest-evaluation.md) and [decisions.md](decisions.md).

## Program Selection

Use `accelerator-programs.json` for initial profiles and official links, then verify the actual cohort, location, stage, deadlines, terms, and required materials. Profiles are lookup aids, not current eligibility certificates. Do not default every startup to YC.

YC and founder-first programs such as Entrepreneurs First may accept applicants before a product, revenue, or a settled idea. Other programs require a company, product, specific location, or stage. Treat these as program-specific rules, not universal prerequisites. Distinguish Sequoia's open call from Arc's intensive for Sequoia-backed companies; inspect current official details.

## Assessment

Ask only what changes the decision:

1. What is being made, or which founder capability is being evaluated at pre-idea stage?
2. Who has the problem, and what do they do today instead?
3. What evidence exists, and what is still a hypothesis?
4. What relevant founder advantage and recent execution are demonstrated?
5. Does the business ambition fit this program and the user's goal?
6. Which mandatory conditions pass, fail, or remain unknown?
7. What is the strongest reason for and against applying now, including the time cost and deadline?

Lead with a decisive incompatibility when present. Absence of traction is an evidence gap, not an eligibility failure unless the program explicitly requires it.

## Application Review

Replace marketing adjectives with facts, make founder edge concrete, and distinguish shipped work, user claims, observed usage, payment, and plans. Founder-first applications may rely on demonstrated ability and insight without inventing customer proof.

Separate mandatory checks from the quality rubric. For `apply` + `proceed`, verify `program_eligibility` and `required_materials` with evidence. A high weighted score cannot cancel a failed condition. Drafting can finish as `artifact` + `complete` while application readiness remains unresolved.

## Improvement Plan

Select the smallest gap that can realistically change the decision before the deadline. Specify participants or artifacts, access, metric, rationale for the threshold, inconclusive outcome, and time budget. Do not impose seven days or 10–20 interviews on every project. Annual renewal, enterprise sales, or recruiting a cofounder have different learning cycles.

## Output

Give program fit, the scoped decision, mandatory conditions, relevant strengths and weaknesses, missing proof, answer fixes or interview risks, and an improvement plan when needed. List the official sources checked and their dates. Numerical rubric colors describe quality only; they do not override eligibility.
