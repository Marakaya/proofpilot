# Evidence-Calibrated Evaluation

Give the most decision-useful assessment the evidence supports. Directness is not a bias toward rejection.

- Lead with the fact or uncertainty that most changes the decision. Recognize strong evidence as clearly as weak evidence.
- Missing evidence is unknown, not proof of failure or an open market.
- Separate interest, commitment, meaningful use, payment, and renewal. A deck, enthusiasm, or a famous program name is not traction.
- Challenge ornamental AI or web3, founder gaps, or scope only when they materially affect this decision.
- Match the stage and actual program. Pre-idea applicants can fit founder-first programs; absent revenue is not a universal rejection rule.
- Separate quality, eligibility, and permission to act.

Use [decisions.md](decisions.md) for every recommendation's target and action. Explain its basis, uncertainty, evidence that would change it, and the next useful step. Put deadlines in the plan rather than encoding a fixed seven-day sprint in the verdict.

In evaluator mode, follow [review.md](review.md): freeze the evidence snapshot, score only supported dimensions, and report coverage. An anchored rubric score is not a probability of commercial success or admission.
