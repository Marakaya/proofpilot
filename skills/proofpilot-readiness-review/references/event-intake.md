# Event Submission Form

Generate this in the user's language, adapted to the frozen event policy. Five short sections are sufficient; accept links to existing artifacts rather than asking teams to rewrite them. Workshop applicants do not need customer research or a startup pitch unless that is the assignment.

## 1. Project And Task

- Project/team name and the assessed team or individual.
- Who uses it, what task/problem it addresses and how the task is handled now.
- What improves and why the event's technology is useful.
- For a workshop: the assigned exercise and what the participant completed.
- For a product hackathon: a practical next test and route to initial users/support.

## 2. Submitted Code

- Repository access and the commit/version submitted by the deadline.
- Short README: entry points, dependencies, setup and known limitations.
- What existed before the event, what was created during it, reused components and AI assistance under the event rules.
- Team responsibilities. Commit volume is not an effort score.

## 3. Repeatable Scenario

- App/demo URL or CLI/API/local instructions.
- Test data and steps with expected results; test-account access through an appropriate private channel if needed.
- What really runs, what is mocked and what is unfinished.
- For relevant blockchain work: network, programs/integrations and an example transaction locator.

Do not request private keys, seed phrases, production-user records or a judge's personal funds. Local/testnet demonstration is acceptable when the event permits it.

## 4. Demonstration And Explanation

- One short scenario video (suggested 3–5 minutes; the event's own limit takes precedence).
- A transcript or plain-text narration, plus timestamps for the important actions. This is a fallback for inaccessible media and can be generated, then checked by the team.
- Slides if already used; no separate polished deck required by default.
- Q&A answers or judge observations when available, especially for understanding in a workshop.

If a runtime cannot inspect a medium, request an equivalent accessible artifact and record the limitation; do not penalize a team for the evaluator's tool failure. Any additions follow the common evidence cutoff/remedy policy.

## 5. Claims And Evidence

For two or three material claims, supply: the claim, its basis, what remains uncertain and what changed because of the finding. A code behavior, prototype test or integration result can serve this purpose; customer interviews are not universally required.

For interview/research claims, use anonymized excerpts or a compact account of recruitment, questions, contrary findings and changes. A self-reported summary remains self-reported. No mandatory sample size, revenue threshold, full private recordings or personal contact list.

Honest `not yet tested` is acceptable input. It limits supported conclusions, without cancelling unrelated technical achievements.
